# Process record G0: How the research questions were identified

Neutral record of the phase that produced the questions R1, R2, R4, R5 and Q0.2. Covers the first
gap map, the assessment of the pre-existing gap document, the isolated second pass, and the
three-way comparison. Part A is a one-page summary. Part B is the full trail. Result documents:
`../splash-pool-flexibility.md`, `../perspectives_splash.md`, `../splash-what-how-when.md`.
Worked 2026-09-08.

---

## Part A. One-page method summary

**Question.** For a one-year project on swimming pools as power-to-heat flexibility in
Schleswig-Holstein, what does the scholarly literature not know, as distinct from what the project
merely has to collect? And is a pre-existing gap document pointing in the right direction?

**Method, three stages.** First, a topic-mode literature and policy review run against the job
advertisement, producing a five-gap map with every finding labelled. Second, an assessment of the
user's existing 286-line gap document against that map and against the verification method. Third,
an independent second pass: a multi-perspective scan run in an isolated context that was given the
advertisement and the method files but was explicitly forbidden from reading the first map, so
that agreement between the two would mean something. Finally a three-way comparison, with the
second pass's headline finding independently verified before acceptance.

**Result.** Five research gaps: fleet-scale aggregation and curtailment attribution; whether
distribution-connected loads can reach the binding constraint at all; why an available regulatory
instrument goes unused; the comfort and hygiene envelope as a formal flexibility bound; and
willingness under real municipal governance. The second pass added three the first missed: the
reference accounting's structural zero for baths, the evaporation loop, and the 2022/23 temperature
episode as a natural experiment. The pre-existing document was assessed as the right territory at
the wrong altitude, with roughly 100 unresolvable citation markers.

**Status.** Curtailment figures, the regulatory instrument's rules and the modelling stack
VERIFIED from primary sources. Absence claims UNRESOLVED, by both passes' own admission, because
neither ran a systematic search protocol.

**Files.** `splash-pool-flexibility.md` and its provenance; `perspectives_splash.md`;
`splash-gaps_ORIGINAL.md` (the assessed document); `splash-what-how-when.md`;
`verification_log.md` first and second passes; corrections C1 to C4.

## Part B. Full trail

### B.1 Framing

1. The inputs were the job advertisement (verbatim), a 286-line gap document written previously by
   the candidate, and an instruction to produce a research-gap map only, without interview
   framing.
2. Two framing decisions were taken at the start and recorded in the plan. First, the target is a
   domain switch away from the default research domain, so the canon's rule on appending rather
   than overwriting a domain profile applies. Second, and central to everything that followed, a
   working definition: a work task is something the project must build or collect because it does
   not exist locally; a research gap is a question the field cannot answer, whose answer
   generalises beyond Schleswig-Holstein. Every candidate finding was tested against that
   distinction.
3. The pre-existing document was copied to the programme folder unchanged before any analysis, so
   that the artifact under assessment could not drift.
4. Scale decision, recorded: lead-owned direct search rather than delegation, because the question
   is narrow and specialist and a cold start would cost more than it returns. Minimum three query
   angles per key question.

### B.2 First pass: what the field already knows

5. Nine searches across six angles: pool thermal storage and demand response; municipal pool
   energy consumption in Germany; power-to-heat against curtailment; the German regulatory
   instrument; the Flensburg group's own modelling; and demand-response acceptance among
   institutional operators.
6. The prior work on pools as a demand-response asset was found to be thin, Nordic,
   simulation-based and price-driven: one Finnish swimming hall, an energy-use study of Norwegian
   halls, a pool-as-cooling-storage study, and an aquatic-centre operating-data benchmark. This
   established the opening the project occupies, and corrected the pre-existing document's implicit
   framing that pools are unstudied.
7. Four fetches were attempted on the closest paper and its repository record; both returned 403.
   Its citation was VERIFIED from two independent listings and its quantitative results marked
   UNRESOLVED, a status that persisted through every later pass.
8. The state's grid-congestion report was fetched and produced the pass's load-bearing finding:
   on-land curtailment fell from about 3,350 GWh in 2019 to 825 GWh in 2025, and over 65% of it is
   ordered by the upstream transmission operator for congestion partly outside the state. This
   reframed the advertised geospatial package from a data problem into a question about where in
   the network topology a distribution-connected load can relieve a constraint, and became R2.
9. The regulatory instrument was fetched from an independent assessment: eligibility segments
   including large heat pumps, additionality conditions, a trial from October 2024, and the
   finding that the instrument had essentially not been used despite available supply. This became
   R5's starting point. The reported phase-3 date could not be confirmed (the source deck would not
   parse) and was carried at `[verify]` for six passes until R2 closed it from the operators' own
   concept.

### B.3 First pass: assessing the pre-existing document

10. The document was read in full and assessed on four axes. Structure: its work-package
    decomposition tracks the advertisement accurately, and several individual observations are
    sharp. Altitude: most of its roughly 45 items describe what the project must do rather than
    what the field does not know, which is the central defect; most convert with reframing, and
    examples of the conversion were given. Citations: roughly 100 markers of the form `[web:N]`
    with no source list anywhere in 286 lines, so none resolves and none may enter a deliverable.
    Omissions: the regulatory instrument entirely absent, the curtailment premise not interrogated,
    and the prior pool literature missing.
11. One factual contradiction with the advertisement was found and logged (C2): the document
    describes a half-time post; the advertisement is full-time.

### B.4 The independence problem, and how it was handled

12. The question was raised whether a multi-perspective scan should be run on the gaps. The method
    file's own skip conditions were applied: it says to skip the scan where a single verified
    source is what is needed, and the open items at that point were verification tasks, not
    framing tasks. A further objection was recorded: a scan run in the same context as the first
    map would inherit its framing, and the method warns that personas share one model's priors, so
    their agreement is not confirmation.
13. When an independent pass was nonetheless requested, the isolation was made structural rather
    than a matter of self-restraint. A second pass was run in a fresh context that received only
    the advertisement verbatim and the two method files, and was explicitly forbidden from opening
    the first map, its provenance, its plan, the verification log or the pre-existing document. Web
    search was left unrestricted on the stated reasoning that reaching the same public sources
    independently is convergent evidence, while reading the first pass's conclusions is
    contamination.
14. The second pass confirmed in its output that the isolation held and that it had seen none of
    the first pass's material.

### B.5 Second pass: what it added

15. It ran a five-lens research cast, a contradiction map and a self peer-review, and reported its
    own dominant lens as the skeptic, noting that a scan run to find gaps is structurally
    predisposed to a deficit narrative, and that its practitioner section was its weakest because
    it rested on trade sources with no operating data from a single German pool.
16. Convergence with the first pass, reached independently: the same curtailment figures and their
    upstream cause; the same conclusion that the geospatial package measures the wrong adjacency;
    the same finding that the prior literature is single-asset and price-driven; and the same
    regulatory instrument, though ranked fourth rather than first.
17. Three findings the first pass had missed. A reference demand-response accounting table scoring
    baths at zero flexible electricity for a methodological reason, which became R1. The coupled
    water, humidity and electricity loop, which became R4 and which the first pass had predicted as
    its own likely blind spot in the engineering direction. And the winter 2022/23 fleet-wide
    temperature reduction as an existing natural experiment, which became R3 and which remains
    unworked because it requires operator contact.
18. It also surfaced a detail that later corrected the first pass's reading of the regulatory
    instrument: an exclusion tied to combined heat and power contracts. That correction was itself
    later narrowed when the statute was read directly in R5 (C12).

### B.6 Verifying the second pass before accepting it

19. Its headline finding was checked rather than taken on trust. The source PDF was located and
    text-extracted locally after two browser routes failed, and the table row was read directly.
    The finding was confirmed, and two corrections followed: the claim that baths were the only
    zero was wrong (laundries is also zero), and the citation year and an author's name were wrong.
    Logged as C3 and C4.
20. The second pass had itself logged a correction of the same kind in its own record: it initially
    suspected a search summary of fabricating a claim, then retrieved the full paper and found the
    claim correct and specific, recording the lesson that an abstract-page miss is not disproof.
    This was taken as evidence that the pass was run honestly.

### B.7 Three-way comparison and sequencing

21. The comparison was drawn explicitly, with the epistemic caveat stated: convergence between two
    passes of the same underlying model means a finding is reachable by more than one route, not
    that it is true; the informative signal is the disagreement. Both passes independently failed to
    find any public description of the project itself, so all analysis rests on the advertisement.
22. The gaps were then sequenced against the project calendar into a what, how and when document,
    which introduced the Tier 0 questions (is there an addressable empirical object; does an
    inventory exist) and identified the structural mismatch between six advertised work packages
    and four master's theses.
23. When work moved from identifying gaps to answering them, the order was chosen by what could be
    answered without proprietary data: R1 first (needs only public tables), then R4 (published
    coefficients), then Q0.2 (public administrative records), then R2 (which Q0.2 unblocked), then
    R5 (primary legal texts). R3 remains unworked because it requires contacting operators.

### B.8 How the conclusions were derived

The gap set is the product of three filters applied in order. The work-task versus research-gap
distinction (step 2) removed most of the pre-existing document's items and most of the
advertisement's packages. Primary-source verification (steps 8, 9, 19) removed or re-labelled
findings that could not be traced, including one of the second pass's own headline claims. And
structural independence (step 13) rather than self-restraint was used to test whether the first
pass's framing was an artifact, which it partly was: three of the five worked questions originate
in the second pass, and one of them (R4) was the blind spot the first pass had predicted in itself
but not filled.

### B.9 Final conclusion

The project as advertised is largely a collection exercise; its research content lies underneath
it, in five questions. Two passes run independently agree on the two structural ones (the
constraint geography, and fleet scale versus single asset) and disagree usefully on the rest, with
the second pass supplying the methodological gap that became the strongest single result of the
whole exercise. The pre-existing gap document is the right territory at the wrong altitude, with
citations that cannot be used; its structure and several of its observations survive reframing.
The largest identified gap, what public-service quality is spent per unit of flexibility and which
user group bears it, remains unworked and is scoped as beyond a one-year project.

**Status of the conclusion.** VERIFIED as to the primary-source findings that anchor each gap.
UNRESOLVED as to every absence claim, by both passes' own admission: the correct formulation is
"not found", never "does not exist". The gap set is a set of options, not a selection; choosing
among them is not this method's function.

### B.10 What would change it

A documented systematic search protocol, which would convert the absence claims from UNRESOLVED to
either established or refuted; a public description of the project, which neither pass could find;
and reading the closest prior paper in full, which has been blocked at every attempt and which
bears on at least two of the five gaps.
