# Process records: SPLASH research

Eight records of how each question was worked. Each has the same two-part shape: **Part A** is a
one-page method summary (question, sources, method, result, status, limits, files); **Part B** is
the full trail, numbered step by step, including the dead ends and the corrections they forced.
Neutral third-person record.

| Record | Question | Conclusion in one line |
|---|---|---|
| [G0](P-G0-gap-identification.md) | How the research questions were identified | Five gaps, from two independently run passes plus an assessment of the pre-existing gap document; three of the five worked questions originate in the isolated second pass |
| [Q0.1](P-Q01-addressable-curtailment.md) | What share of the curtailment can a distribution-connected pool reach? | The question inverts: about 97% is transmission-caused, and that is exactly what §13k makes reachable. The unreachable residual is ~3% and has no instrument |
| [Q0.2](P-Q02-sh-pool-inventory.md) | Does a usable Schleswig-Holstein pool inventory exist? | Yes for counts, locations and ownership, current to 2025 and reconciled at 99.93%. No for technical attributes, and the state says so itself |
| [R1](P-R1-electrification-conditional-flexibility.md) | Is the zero flexibility score for baths a property of the asset or the method? | The method. Electrifying pool heat moves baths from 0.0 to 0.30-0.97 TWh/a of theoretical flexible electricity under the method's own rules |
| [R2](P-R2-locational-value.md) | Does proximity to wind predict where curtailed energy can be claimed? | No. 32% agreement across 76 indoor units; the pools closest to the wind are the excluded ones |
| [R4](P-R4-evaporation-loop.md) | What does the evaporation loop cost when storing heat in pool water? | It sets the regime, not viability. 2-3% overnight; about a third of shifted electricity when held through summer bathing hours |
| [R3](P-R3-premise-and-panel.md) | Can the winter 2022/23 temperature episode serve as a natural experiment? | Not as posed. Public pools were exempt from the regulation, so the cuts were not mandated; a public 2023 and 2025 activity series exists but starts after the episode and needs unit reconciliation |
| [R5](P-R5-dispatch-authority.md) | Who can commit a municipal pool to a grid signal, and does it pay? | Nobody alone: council, operator's hygiene duty and network operator each hold a veto. Single-site economics are marginal outside the best case |

**Not worked to an answer.** R3, the winter 2022/23 temperature episode. Its premise was checked
and corrected and a partial public dataset was found (record R3), but a pre-period requires
contact with pool operators about surviving records and could not be obtained from the desk. It is
the most time-critical of the questions.

**Reading order.** G0 first if the question is how the agenda was set. Otherwise Q0.1, R2 and R5
are the three with the most direct bearing on the project as advertised, and Q0.1 should be read
before R2 because it establishes why relief-region membership is the right variable rather than a
proxy.

**Underlying material.** Result documents and their provenance files sit one level up in
`research/`; seven scripts reproduce every number and run from any working directory;
`.drafts/evidence/` holds 44 source files (PDFs, spreadsheets and their local text extractions) so
that each input can be re-checked without a network; and `../../verification_log.md` records twelve
verification passes and twenty-two numbered corrections, including corrections to this project's own
earlier conclusions.
